# Cold Open: Dexamethasone for Severe COVID-19

In March 2020, as hospitals worldwide faced a staggering 40% mortality rate among mechanically ventilated COVID-19 patients, a team at Oxford University made a controversial decision: they would test a 62-year-old generic steroid that cost pennies per dose—despite the World Health Organization explicitly recommending against it. One hundred days later, on June 16, 2020, their RECOVERY trial announced results that would change medical history: dexamethasone reduced mortality by 36% in ventilated patients, becoming the first proven COVID-19 treatment and saving an estimated 650,000 lives in just six months. This is the story of how systematic science, crisis-driven speed, and a willingness to challenge conventional wisdom transformed a routine hospital medication into the single most impactful therapeutic intervention of the pandemic—and what it teaches us about drug repurposing when lives hang in the balance.
