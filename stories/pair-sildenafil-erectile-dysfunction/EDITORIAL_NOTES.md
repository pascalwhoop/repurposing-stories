## Recommendations for Future Episodes

### Patterns to Watch For

1. **Content Duplication Across Sections**
   - **Issue:** When writer agents work independently on adjacent sections, they may repeat key facts or data points
   - **Prevention:** Have writer review adjacent sections before drafting, or editor actively scan for repeated phrases/stats
   - **Example from this episode:** Financial trajectory appeared verbatim in sections 05 and 07

2. **Forced Break Transitions**
   - **Issue:** Writers may insert "let's take a break" reflexively rather than at true emotional reset points
   - **Prevention:** Break transitions should only occur where emotional intensity needs to reset before rebuilding
   - **Example from this episode:** Break after Chapter 1 (setup) was unnecessary; break after Chapter 2 (failure) was organic

3. **Missing Decision Resolutions**
   - **Issue:** Cliffhanger transitions can create confusion if the listener doesn't know the outcome of a major decision
   - **Prevention:** Before transitioning to consequences, explicitly state the decision/outcome
   - **Example from this episode:** Section 04 ended mid-decision without confirming Pfizer committed to ED indication

4. **Micro vs. Macro Transitions**
   - **Issue:** Jumping from individual patient stories to systemic impact without signaling the zoom-out can feel jarring
   - **Prevention:** Use explicit "let's zoom out" or "bigger picture" language when shifting levels of analysis
   - **Example from this episode:** Section 06 needed explicit framing as systemic/macro view after section 05's patient-level detail

### Quality Checkpoints for Editors

For future episodes, editors should specifically verify:

- [ ] **No verbatim duplication** of key facts/figures across sections
- [ ] **Break transitions** only at genuine emotional reset points (typically after climax or major act transitions)
- [ ] **Decision points** clearly resolved before moving to consequences
- [ ] **Level-of-analysis transitions** explicitly signaled (micro → macro or vice versa)
- [ ] **Financial/market data** consolidated in one comprehensive summary, not scattered across sections
- [ ] **Host dynamics** consistent with showrunner's vision (who leads which topics)
- [ ] **Through-lines** maintained (e.g., tissue specificity thread from Chapter 1 setup → Chapter 3 revelation → Chapter 5 recap)
